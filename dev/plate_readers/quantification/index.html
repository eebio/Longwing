Quantification

There are a variety of methods for quantifying exact concentrations from fluorescence for some specific types of experiments. Each method uses the quantification(data, time Method()) function signature. These can be used in the transformations in the Excel template.

Longwing.quantification — Function
quantification(data, time, method::AbstractQuantificationMethod;
    plot_directory = nothing)

Convert DNA/RNA fluorescence measurements to absolute units (e.g. concentration).

Arguments

  • data: a DataFrame of fluorescence measurements.
  • time: a DataFrame of times for the fluorescence measurements.
  • method: the method to use for quantifying fluorescence. The methods match the Clearissa software, and require a specific structure for the experimental design. See the documentation for each method (or the Clearissa software documentation) for details.

Keyword Arguments

  • plot_directory: the directory to save plots to. If nothing, no plots are saved. If :temp, a temporary directory is created and used. Otherwise, describes a directory path to save plots to.

TMSD

Longwing.TMSD — Type
TMSD

Convert arbitrary fluorescence units to a concentration (in nM) for Toehold Mediated Strand Displacement (TMSD) reactions.

Arguments:

  • blank: a DataFrame of blank fluorescence measurements.
  • positive_controls: a DataFrame of positive control fluorescence measurements.
  • init_time: a tuple of the form (starttime, endtime) describing the time window to use for the initialisation phase.
  • saturation_time: a tuple of the form (starttime, endtime) describing the time window to use for the saturation phase.
  • positive_control_concentrations: a Dict mapping the names of the positive control wells to their concentrations in nM.

Reaction has the form: B + A-x -> A-B + x

where:

  • B is the invader strand
  • A-x is the substrate complex
  • A-B is the product complex

Total fluorescence is assumed to be of the form: h(t) = [A-B] + β[A-x]

This trajectory is estimated using: h(t) = (fraw(t) - fblank(t)) / pcorr(t) * Cref where:

  • f_raw(t) is the raw fluorescence measurement being quantified
  • f_blank(t) is the blank fluorescence measurement (calibration uses TimeseriesBlank)
  • p_corr(t) is the positive control calibration trajectory
  • C_ref is the concentration of the positive control

For TMSD, C_ref is not relevant since it cancels with the positive control calibration trajectory.

β is estimated as the ratio of the mean of h(t) over the initialisation phase (inittime) and [A-x]₀, the initial concentration of the substrate complex, estimated as the mean of h(t) over the saturation phase (saturationtime), since saturation should mean the reaction has gone to completion and all of the substrate complex has been converted to product complex.

Since [A-x]₀ = [A-B] + [A-x], we have that: [A-B] = (h(t) - β[A-x]₀) / (1 - β) which is used to compute the final product concentration per well.

HMSD

Longwing.HMSD — Type
HMSD

Convert arbitrary fluorescence units to a concentration (in nM) for Handhold Mediated Strand Displacement (HMSD) reactions.

Arguments:

  • blank: a DataFrame of blank fluorescence measurements.
  • positive_controls: a DataFrame of positive control fluorescence measurements.
  • reference: a DataFrame of reference fluorescence measurements.
  • init_time: a tuple of the form (starttime, endtime) describing the time window to use for the initialisation phase.
  • positive_control_concentrations: a Dict mapping the names of the positive control wells to their concentrations in nM.
  • concentration_reference: the concentration of the substrate-template in the reference control in nM.

Reaction has the form: S1-T + R1 -> S1-T-R1

where:

  • S1-T is the substrate-template
  • R1 is the reporter
  • S1-T-R1 is the product complex

Total fluorescence is assumed to be of the form: h(t) = [S1-T] + β[S1-T-R1]

This trajectory is estimated using: h(t) = (fraw(t) - fblank(t)) / (fref(t) - fblank(t)) * C_ref where:

  • f_raw(t) is the raw fluorescence measurement being quantified
  • f_blank(t) is the blank fluorescence measurement (calibration uses TimeseriesBlank)
  • f_ref(t) is the reference fluorescence measurement
  • C_ref is the concentration of the substrate-template in the reference control

β is estimated as the ratio of the mean of pcorr(t) over the initialisation phase (inittime) and the mean of fref(t) - fblank(t) over the initialisation phase. This value of β is used for the whole plate.

Since [S1-T]₀ = [S1-T] + [S1-T-R1], we have that: [S1-T-R1] = ([S1-T]₀ - h(t)) / (1 - β) which is used to compute the final product concentration per well.

Internal TMSD

Longwing.InternalTMSD — Type
InternalTMSD

Convert arbitrary fluorescence units to a concentration (in nM) for Internal Toehold Mediated Strand Displacement (Internal TMSD) reactions.

Arguments:

  • blank: a DataFrame of blank fluorescence measurements.
  • positive_controls: a DataFrame of positive control fluorescence measurements.
  • negative_controls: a DataFrame of negative control fluorescence measurements.
  • init_time: a tuple of the form (starttime, endtime) describing the time window to use for the initialisation phase.
  • saturation_time: a tuple of the form (starttime, endtime) describing the time window to use for the saturation phase.
  • stable_neg_time: a tuple of the form (starttime, endtime) describing the time window to use for the stable negative phase.
  • positive_control_concentrations: a Dict mapping the names of the positive control wells to their concentrations in nM.
  • trigger_time: the time at which the reaction is triggered. All values before this time are removed from the final output. Should be after the initialisation phase.

Reaction has the form: A-B-T + F -> A-B-F + T

where:

  • A-B-T is the substrate
  • F is the invader/fuel
  • A-B-F is the fluorescent product
  • T is the template

Total fluorescence is assumed to be of the form: h(t) = [A-B-F] + β[A-B-T]

This trajectory is estimated using: h(t) = (fraw(t) - fblank(t)) / pcorr(t) * Cref where:

  • f_raw(t) is the raw fluorescence measurement being quantified
  • f_blank(t) is the blank fluorescence measurement (calibration uses TimeseriesBlank)
  • p_corr(t) is the positive control calibration trajectory
  • C_ref is the concentration of the positive control

For InternalTMSD, C_ref is not relevant since it cancels with the positive control calibration trajectory.

[A-B-T]₀, the initial concentration of the substrate complex, is estimated as the mean of h(t) over the saturation phase (saturation_time), since saturation should mean the reaction has gone to completion and all of the substrate complex has been converted to product complex.

The negative controls are used to estimate β, which is the ratio of the mean of h(t) over the stable negative phase (stablenegtime) and the median of [A-B-T]₀ across all wells.

Since [A-B-T]₀ = [A-B-T] + [A-B-F], we have that: [A-B-F] = (h(t) - β[A-B-T]₀) / (1 - β) which is used to compute the final product concentration per well.

Relative Fluorescence

Longwing.RelativeFluorescence — Type
RelativeFluorescence

Convert arbitrary fluorescence units to a relative fluorescence value (where 0 is defined by a negative control and 1 is defined by positive controls).

Arguments:

  • blank: a DataFrame of blank fluorescence measurements.
  • positive_controls: a DataFrame of positive control fluorescence measurements.
  • negative_controls: a DataFrame of negative control fluorescence measurements.
  • init_time: a tuple of the form (starttime, endtime) describing the time window to use for the initialisation phase.
  • stable_neg_time: a tuple of the form (starttime, endtime) describing the time window to use for the stable negative phase.
  • positive_control_concentrations: a Dict mapping the names of the positive control wells to their concentrations in nM.
  • concentration_reference: the concentration of the positive control in nM.

All data is calibrated with the blank timeseries using TimeseriesBlank.

The negative control baseline, f_neg,base, is calculated as the mean of the negative control fluorescence measurements over the stable negative phase.

The relative fluorescence is calculated as: RF(t) = (fraw(t) - fneg,base) / (pcorr(t) - fneg,base) where:

  • f_raw(t) is the raw fluorescence measurement (after calibration) being quantified
  • f_neg,base is the negative control baseline
  • p_corr(t) is the positive control calibration trajectory
